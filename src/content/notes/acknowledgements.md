I owe my thanks to the countless thinkers and tinkerers who have created before me. Ideas are never entirely one's own but embedded in the ideas before them, and the same is true of me. I hope that I too have created something worth creating, as others have for me.

I thank the Santa Fe Institute, SFI Press, and its countless staff and academics for advancing the paradigm of complexity science. Much of my ideas are primarily shaped by SFI. The accessibility and low-cost distribution of their books is the reason I can read their books from the other side of the world. It took three months to ship to the Philippines, and yet their books are worth the lifetimes of accumulated knowledge therein. I would highly recommend their books to anyone interested in complexity science.

I thank Gwern Branwen for his writing, design principles, and numerous experiments on [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/design). If fiddling with gwern.net's CSS for hours on end is an entirely selfish endeavour, then I would add that much humanity has benefited from the selfish endeavours of artists, scientists, and all the like, who in the pursuit of their passions have inspired first themselves, then the world.

I thank Wikimedia Foundation and the contributors to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/)[^1]. I am extremely fortunate my coming-of-age rabbit holes happened to align with the democratization of knowledge, even (or especially) when I read more than I probably should've.

I thank Dave Liepmann, Edward Tufte, and the contributors to [Tufte CSS](https://edwardtufte.github.io/tufte-css/) for the numerous techniques translating Tufte's principles on the display of information and patterns to web.

I thank the team behind Obsidian for the tool that brought me into writing again, and Steph Ango for sharing his philosophy and software for free with the world.

I thank every contributor to every tool I have used, every library, author, and resource I have read from, and everyone who has had a personal effect on me. Their names are too many to list here, so instead they are scattered across the epigraphs, bibliographies, and marginalia of this site.

Lastly, I thank my partner, who has loved me through depression and disability, and is the reason I read and write again.

[^1]"[W]hen in doubt, copy Wikipedia." Gwern Branwen, [Design Of This Website](https://gwern.net/design#principles)
