title: "To study is an act of love for existence"
date: 2025-08-07

The puddles surround me now. The damp draft advected to places I have never met. Bike chains and grass and rim light around the heavens. Brownian bubbles and trickles through concrete capillaries, or maybe crystallines, that eventually percolate through the earth and inevitably flow again into life. The same life that observes, that sits here, to write about the puddles and draft and the organism of existence.

I am in awe of being, of what has been, and what will become of it all.

Why must non-fiction be defined by what is not? Human history so often enjoys tales of what could be and not what is. I have observed, but never fully understood, how we often overlook the spectacle of mundane reality, the ineffable awe of this blink of the cosmic calendar.

It is rough on the ground where I sit with the half-eaten *tenga ng baboy, tatlong palito, dalawang sawsawan, isang panaghoy sa diwa*. *Kani-kaninong hininga at dugo ang dumaloy dito, at kanino ang darating pa?*[^1]

Tonight I will read the lightning as Morse code. Or build Legos after structural engineering. Or frustrate myself with the limitations of the latent manifold of cognition. Or visualize the world under, the world of, the world otherwise, the topological meshes, the harmony dissection, the semiotics of language, the natural fractals. The philosophy and philalethia.

There is so much to love. Attention is all you need.

To study is an act of love for existence.

[^1]: Translation: It is rough on the ground where I sit with a half-eaten pig's ear, three bare skewers, two dipping sauces, and a spirit anguished by the limits of what I know. Whose breath and blood have flowed here, and whose are yet to come?
