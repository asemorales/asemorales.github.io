> [!quote]Blaise Pascal, *[Pensées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pens%C3%A9es)*
> The will is one of the chief factors in belief, not that it creates belief, but because things are true or false according to the aspect in which we look at them. The will, which prefers one aspect to another, turns away the mind from considering the qualities of all that it does not like to see; and thus the mind, moving in accord with the will, stops to consider the aspect which it likes, and so judges by what it sees.

One's relationship to acquiring knowledge determines your relationship to accepting and filtering reality, since epistemology is the lens through which we filter all meaning-making about reality.

Importantly, this differs from one's relationship to using and accessing knowledge, which defines how one acts upon reality. In contrast, epistemology is one's relationship to acquiring and integrating knowledge, such that reality acts upon oneself.
