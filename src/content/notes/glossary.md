This glossary defines terms used across these notes. Each entry gives the word, its part of speech, and its meanings, and some entries also give its origin.

## emergence (noun)

The appearance of properties in a whole that its parts do not have on their own.

## entelechy (noun)

1. The realization of a thing's potential, as opposed to the potential alone.
2. In vitalist doctrines, a force said to direct the development of an organism.

### Etymology

From Greek *entelékheia*, a word coined by Aristotle.
