---
title: "Understand the system, don't solve a problem"
date: 2025-11-30
updated: 2026-11-07
tags: ["complexity"]
---

If a problem consistently arises in a system, reality is giving you feedback that this is not a problem in the system, but an error in your interpretation of the system: [the purpose of a system is what it does](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_purpose_of_a_system_is_what_it_does). [[everything is rational in itself]] in that it is allowed for by reality, and therefore all "problems" are systems working exactly as they are designed. You seeing a phenomenon as a problem presupposes that you are starting from a faulty mental model of how the system "should" work, rather than starting from a mental model of "why" the system works the way it does. Therefore, to "fix" a problem, one must ironically first let go of viewing it as such: don't solve a problem, seek to understand the system.
