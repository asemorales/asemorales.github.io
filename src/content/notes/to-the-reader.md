> [!quote] Michel de Montaigne, *[Essays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essays_%28Montaigne%29)*
> I myself am the subject of my book: it is not reasonable that you should employ your leisure on a topic so frivolous and so vain.

In a word, this site is an autobiography. It as an experiment in creating [living documentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_document) of [[glossary#entelechy (noun)|entelechy]] within myself.

By that, I mean to say that I am a [computationally irreducible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_irreducibility) [complex system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system). At any given moment I am simultaneously in a certain state, and unfolding towards future possible states[^1] within the adjacent possible. I am unable to predict my future states, although I am guilty for having had the intellectual arrogance to try and do so for much of my life.  

have tried and failed to for much of my life.

Hence a new approach  

All my writings are an experiment in documenting who I am by articulating my current ideas, and allowing my ideas to unfold. I am my ideas. As I change, my ideas change too, and vice versa.

In other books, this section might be titled "About the Author," and I almost named it so. But I am fundamentally unable to tell you about myself, for any narrative I give in an "About the author" page is a distilled mental model I have about myself at my current state. That narrative would be both inaccurate (for my mental model of myself is biased and limited) and incomplete (for in describing only my current state I deprive you of the opportunity to observing the complexity any complex system holds). Hence I have decided not to hand you my self-model through some limited narrative, and instead invite you to create your own narrative, your own mental model of me, from my writing. This entire site, after all, is "About the Author."

I write primarily for myself[^2], as an exercise in rigor to develop my thoughts, for [[rigor develops precision]]. It is also a memory aid for when my future self will forget me, for writing is an extension of memory[^4]. My writing is an entirely selfish[^3] endeavor.

I believe that [[the evolution of ideas are biographies]].
