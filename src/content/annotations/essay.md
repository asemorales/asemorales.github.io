**essay**, an [analytic](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/analytic), interpretative, or critical literary [composition](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/composition). The French writer [Michel de Montaigne](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Michel-de-Montaigne) chose the name *essai* to emphasize that his [compositions](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/compositions) were attempts or endeavours, a groping toward the expression of his personal thoughts and experiences, and as a means of self-discovery. His *[Essais](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Essays-by-Montaigne)* was published in 1588.
